seoFaqs:
  - q: Can I install the cover from inside?
    a: Most window units are covered from outside for a weather-tight seal. Indoor slip covers are available for climate-controlled storage rooms.
  - q: Will the cover block my window?
    a: The cover fits over the cooler cabinet only. It does not replace interior window insulation unless you specify a full-frame panel.
  - q: How do I measure a window cooler?
    a: Measure the exterior cabinet width, depth from wall to outermost point, and total height including any lift lid.
