seoFaqs:
  - q: Should I cover my evaporative cooler in winter?
    a: Yes. Off-season covering keeps freezing water out of pads and panels, prevents debris from clogging the float valve, and protects the motor housing from corrosion.
  - q: Do I need to drain the cooler before installing a cover?
    a: Always drain the reservoir and let internal components dry for 24 hours before covering. Trapping moisture under a cover encourages rust and mildew.
  - q: How do I measure for a custom cover?
    a: Measure width, depth, and height of the cabinet at the widest points, and note the position of any side ducts or top vents so the cover can be cut to clear them.
  - q: What fabric weight should I choose?
    a: 300D polyester suits mild climates; 600D or PVC-laminated fabric is recommended for desert sun, coastal salt air, or rooftop installations exposed to constant wind.
  - q: How much does an evaporative cooler cover cost?

Evaporative cooler covers shield your unit during the off-season and between cooling cycles, when accumulated dust, falling debris, and prolonged UV exposure cause most premature wear. A properly fitted cover keeps pads, motor, and louvers protected so the unit starts cleanly next season.
