seoFaqs:
  - q: How much warmer does insulation keep the cabinet?
    a: Insulation moderates swing rather than heating the unit. It blocks wind chill and reduces frost formation on internal surfaces when the unit is dry and off.
  - q: Is insulated the same as winter covers?
    a: Winter covers emphasize freeze and snow protection; insulated covers focus on thermal mass. Many winter SKUs include both features.
  - q: Do insulated covers cost more?
    a: Yes — additional material and lamination steps add cost. Volume pricing applies on multi-unit orders.
  - q: Can insulation compress in storage?
    a: Store folded loosely; do not stack heavy items on insulated covers long term to preserve loft.
