seoFaqs:
  - q: Will mesh stop fine dust?
    a: Mesh blocks larger debris and pests. Fine desert dust may pass — use a solid 600D cover for off-season sealing.
  - q: Can I run the cooler with a mesh cover on?
    a: Only if the cover is designed for operating clearance and does not restrict intake paths. Most mesh covers are for idle units with airflow needs.
  - q: How do I clean a mesh cover?
    a: Hose down with cool water and mild soap; air-dry fully before storage. Avoid machine agitation that can snag the mesh.
  - q: Mesh vs vented solid cover?
    a: Mesh is fully breathable. Vented solid covers combine fabric panels with screened ports — better rain resistance with some airflow.
