seoFaqs:
  - q: Will an insulated cover prevent pipe freeze?
    a: It reduces heat loss and blocks wind chill on the cabinet. Always drain water lines per your winterization checklist — the cover is one layer of protection.
  - q: Can I use a winter cover in summer?
    a: Solid insulated covers are for off-season use. For summer debris protection, use mesh or vented covers instead.
  - q: How thick is the insulation?
    a: Typical foam cores range 3–5 mm depending on fabric package. Specify your climate zone for a material recommendation.
  - q: When should I remove the winter cover?
    a: Remove before startup in spring, inspect pads and pan, then run the unit before the cooling season peak.
