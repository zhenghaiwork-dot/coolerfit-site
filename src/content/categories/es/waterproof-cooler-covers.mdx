  - q: What is the difference between waterproof and water-resistant?
    a: Water-resistant fabric sheds light rain; waterproof covers with sealed seams handle standing water and driving rain. See our rain-proof feature page for construction detail.
  - q: Do waterproof covers trap moisture inside?
    a: Only if the unit is covered while wet inside. Drain and dry the cooler first; vented hems allow slow breathability on most models.
  - q: What waterproof rating should I look for?
    a: Our premium line targets 5000 mm hydrostatic head on PVC-backed polyester. Specify exposure class if the unit is in direct monsoon or coastal spray.

    a: Yes. PVC-backed covers are stiffer than 600D oxford. Plan for stronger tie-downs on rooftop spans.
