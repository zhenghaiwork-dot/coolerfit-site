seoFaqs:
  - q: How is side-draft different from down-draft?
    a: Side-draft units pull air through a wall face; down-draft units discharge downward from the roof. Cover shape and opening placement differ — specify your draft type when ordering.
  - q: Should side vents be covered or screened?
    a: Off-season solid covers are typical. Screened vents are available if you need airflow while keeping debris out.
  - q: What if my water line exits the side?
    a: Mark the pipe location on your dimension sheet. We position a reinforced opening or notch so the cover does not kink the line.
  - q: Can I use a universal cover on a side-draft unit?
    a: Universal covers often gap at the wall line. A measured custom cover prevents wind flap and water intrusion at the hem.
