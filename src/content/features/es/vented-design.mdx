faqs:
  - q: Will vents let rain in?
    a: Screened vents may admit fine mist in driving rain. Use solid rain-proof covers during storm season; switch to vented when weather is dry.
  - q: Is vented the same as mesh?
    a: Mesh covers are fully breathable. Vented solid covers use fabric panels plus discrete screened openings.
  - q: Can I add vents to a custom cover?
    a: Yes. Mark desired vent locations on your order photos; we reinforce edges to prevent tear propagation.
  - q: Do vented covers help with smell or mildew?
    a: Airflow reduces trapped moisture that feeds mildew on organic debris. The unit must still be dry inside before covering.

Solid covers excel at sealing out winter weather; vented designs add screened ports or full mesh panels so air can move while leaves, birds, and insects stay out. The right choice depends on climate, season, and whether the unit is operating or idle.
