sections:
  - heading: How UV Damages Cooler Covers
    body: Ultraviolet light breaks polymer chains in polyester and vinyl, causing stiffness, cracking, and color shift. Horizontal top panels see the highest dose — especially on down-draft and rooftop installs with no shade.
    bullets:
      - South- and west-facing exposures degrade fastest
      - Dark colors absorb more heat than silver or white tops
      - Chalking on the fabric surface is an early replace indicator
      - Interior pads are not protected unless the cabinet shell is shielded

Untreated polyester on a rooftop cooler can chalk and tear within two seasons in desert sun. UV protection combines stabilizers in the coating, fade-resistant dyes, and optional reflective top panels so the cover — and the cabinet beneath — sees less thermal stress.
