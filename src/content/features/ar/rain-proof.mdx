  - q: Will a rain-proof cover trap humidity inside?
    a: Only if the unit is covered while wet inside. Drain, dry 24 hours, then install. Vented hems on some models allow slow breathability.
  - q: What waterproof rating do you target?
    a: Premium PVC-backed lines target 5000 mm hydrostatic head. Specify your exposure class on the quote form.
  - q: Can rain-proof covers handle snow load?
    a: They shed meltwater but are not insulated for freeze. Use winter or insulated covers where ice accumulation is heavy.
  - q: How do I clean a waterproof cover?
    a: Wipe with mild soap and water; avoid petroleum solvents that attack PVC. Air-dry fully before folding.
