sections:
  - heading: Pre-Installation Checklist
    body: Confirm the cooler is off, water drained, and pads are dry. Remove debris from the cabinet top and verify your cover orientation (tagged front/back if applicable). Inspect tie-down straps, drawcords, and grommets before climbing to a rooftop unit.
    bullets:
      - Power off at disconnect — never cover a running unit
      - Drain pan and water lines; dry 24 hours minimum
      - Clear leaves and grit from the cabinet top
      - Lay out cover and identify front, back, and opening alignment
  - heading: Basic Installation Steps
    body: Drape the cover over the cabinet starting from the highest point on down-draft units (or from the back on side-draft wall units). Pull the hem evenly around all sides before tightening. Secure the elastic hem or drawcord last so fabric seats flat without twisting.
    bullets:
      - 'Down-draft: align top panel first, then pull skirt down over corners'
      - 'Side-draft: fit over top, then work hem under bottom flange'
      - 'Zippered openings: close zippers after hem is positioned'
      - Stand back and check for bunched fabric that can hold water
  - heading: Rooftop Installation
    body: Use fall protection and a helper when possible. Route tie-downs to structural anchors — not loose conduit or pipe straps. Corner webbing on rooftop-grade covers accepts bungee or ratchet straps; avoid overtightening which can tear grommets.
    bullets:
      - OSHA-compliant ladder and harness for roof work
      - Four-point tie-down in high-wind zones
      - Reflective top panel faces up — verify orientation
      - Do not trap standing water — confirm drainage grommets are open
  - heading: Securing in High Winds
    body: In sustained winds above 40 mph (64 km/h), use all provided tie-down points and choose 600D or heavier fabric. Cross straps over the top can supplement corner ties on extra-large covers. Inspect after major storms and retighten when fabric relaxes.
    bullets:
      - Ratchet straps preferred over single bungees for large commercial covers
      - Avoid sharp edges — use corner protectors if straps contact metal flashing
      - Re-tighten 48 hours after first install — fabric relaxes initially
      - Remove cover before high-wind events if not rated for your exposure class
---

Proper installation keeps covers in place through wind and weather without stressing seams. Always shut down and disconnect power before covering, drain the reservoir, and let internal components dry 24 hours to prevent mildew under the fabric.
