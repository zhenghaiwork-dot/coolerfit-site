  - heading: Material Selection
    body: 'Start with environment: desert sun → UV-stabilized 600D or reflective silver topcoat; coastal salt air → heavier PU or TPU lamination; heavy rain → PVC-backed or fully sealed vinyl; winter off-season → insulated laminate. Indoor or warehouse storage may only need 300D polyester.'
    bullets:
      - '600D Oxford + PU: default for most custom residential and light commercial jobs'
      - '900D + TPU: industrial and extra-large cabinets'
      - '300D + PVC backing: maximum waterproofing at moderate weight'
      - 'Mesh: in-season pest and debris barrier — not a winter solution'
  - heading: Material Samples
    body: For repeat B2B buyers and new commercial accounts, we can ship swatches of shortlisted fabrics before production. Samples help facilities teams approve color, stiffness, and coating hand-feel for brand guidelines.
    bullets:
      - Request swatches when specifying a new material code for the first time
      - Include your climate zone and installation type (rooftop, wall, window)
      - Logo printing samples available on 600D and vinyl bases
      - Note fire-retardant or chemical-resistant requirements at quote stage
  - heading: Performance Ratings
    body: Compare materials on waterproof rating (mm H₂O), UV stability, abrasion resistance, and operating temperature — not denier alone. A 600D UV-stabilized fabric often outlasts untreated 900D in direct sun.
    bullets:
      - 'Water-resistant (PU): sheds rain and dust; breathable hem recommended'
      - 'Waterproof (sealed seams + PVC): for standing water and snow load regions'
      - 'UPF 50+ coatings: for rooftop and southwest exposure'
      - 'Insulated (foam core): freeze protection and draft blocking in winter'

Custom orders let you pair fabric weight, coatings, and colors to each job site. Contractors often standardize on one 600D PU spec across a fleet while specifying 900D or vinyl for exposed rooftops. We can hold the same material code across reorders for multi-property maintenance programs.
