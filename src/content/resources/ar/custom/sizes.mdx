  - heading: Minimum and Maximum Sizes
    body: We produce from single small window units up to large industrial evaporative coolers exceeding 50 in. per side. Extremely compact or oversized units may require reinforced panels or panelized construction — flagged at quote review.
    bullets:
      - 'Small window units: often 20–30 in. per side — specify frame overlap needs'

      - 'Commercial / industrial: 45–60+ in. — reinforced stress points included'
      - No strict minimum quantity on small-batch custom line
  - heading: Irregular Shapes
    body: Round cylinders, tapered hoods, and offset duct bumps are common on older swamp coolers. We flat-pattern or segment covers with darts, gussets, or zippered access panels so the fabric does not fight the geometry.
    bullets:
      - 'Round: provide diameter at the widest point plus straight-side height'
      - 'Offset vents: mark XY position from a consistent corner on your photo'
      - Split corners for junction boxes — specify side and height from base
      - See opening design options for duct and service panel cutouts

Every cooler model has a unique cabinet outline. Custom sizing removes the guesswork of one-size-fits-all retail covers. Send outside W×D×H dimensions (or diameter × height for round units) and we cut patterns with appropriate slack, hem style, and tie-down placement.
