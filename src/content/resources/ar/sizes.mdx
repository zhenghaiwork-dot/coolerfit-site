Cooler covers are sized by outside cabinet dimensions — width × depth × height — not pad size or model name alone. Manufacturers build in 1–2 inches (25–50 mm) of leeway so covers slide over hardware, pad frames, and minor model variations. When your measurements fall between two sizes, choose the larger cover and tighten with drawcord or straps.
