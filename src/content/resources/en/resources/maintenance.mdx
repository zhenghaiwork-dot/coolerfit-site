  - heading: Regular Cleaning
    body: Brush off dry dust monthly during the covered season. Wash once per year (or before off-season storage) with mild soap and cool water — never bleach unless the fabric supplier specifies it. Rinse thoroughly and air-dry fully before folding.
    bullets:
      - 'Polyester / canvas: soft brush + mild detergent, cold water rinse'
      - 'Vinyl: wipe with damp cloth; avoid petroleum solvents'
      - 'Mesh: hose down gently; do not machine wash with agitators'
      - 'Insulated: spot-clean when possible to preserve foam core'
  - heading: Stain Removal
    body: Treat bird droppings and sap promptly — they etch coatings if baked in sun. Use a soft cloth and diluted mild detergent; for stubborn marks on vinyl, a dedicated marine/vinyl cleaner is safer than household solvents.
    bullets:
      - Act within 48 hours on organic stains
      - No chlorine bleach on PU-coated polyester
      - Test cleaner on an inside hem first
      - Rinse soap residue — it attracts dust when dry
  - heading: Seasonal Storage
    body: Fold loosely along existing creases; do not compress insulated covers under heavy items. Store in a breathable bag or box in a dry, rodent-free space. Silica gel packs help in humid warehouses.
    bullets:
      - Ensure cover is 100% dry before bagging
      - Avoid hot attics above 120°F (49°C) for PVC-backed fabrics
      - Hang large commercial covers on a rod if folding creases stress seams
      - Label bag with size and model for faster reinstall next season
  - heading: Repair Tips
    body: Small tears in polyester can be patched with outdoor fabric repair tape or sewn with UV-rated thread. Replace torn grommets rather than pulling straps through ripped holes. Seams showing white PU failure may need professional re-coating or replacement on high-value commercial covers.
    bullets:
      - 'Tape patches: round the corners of patch pieces to prevent peel'
      - Restitch loose hems before they propagate
      - Replace missing drawcord before wind season
      - Contact us for bulk repair or replacement on fleet accounts
---

Covers last longer when cleaned before storage and kept dry between seasons. Mud, bird droppings, and tree sap break down PU coatings over time. Follow material-specific cleaning to avoid stripping waterproof layers or melting heat-transfer logos.
