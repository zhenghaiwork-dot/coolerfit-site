  - heading: Why Large Covers Need More Ease
    body: A cover for a two-meter cabinet is heavier and harder to lift than one for a small portable unit. Manufacturing tolerance, seam movement, handles, casters, and service panels also add up across a large pattern. Extra ease helps installation, but exposed covers still need straps or buckles so wind cannot work under the hem.
    bullets:
      - A zipper side opening is quick and can use a storm flap for outdoor exposure
      - Hook-and-loop provides frequent access but must be kept clean in dusty sites
      - Lace-up grommets provide adjustable closure for wind and dust
      - Very large or irregular patterns are confirmed before cutting

faqs:
  - q: Can I submit measurements in centimeters?
    a: Yes. Send centimeters, millimeters, or inches and use one measurement system consistently across the quote.
  - q: Can you make a cover for a model that is not listed?
    a: Yes, subject to the confirmed project MOQ. Send outside height, width, and depth, quantity, photos, destination, and notes about protrusions or openings.
  - q: Should I add extra size to my measurements?
    a: No. Send the actual maximum outside cabinet dimensions. We add the controlled fit allowance when the pattern is prepared.
  - q: Do portable cooler heights include casters?
    a: Published heights often include wheels, but listings vary. Measure the installed unit and state whether the cover should stop above the casters or extend toward the floor.
