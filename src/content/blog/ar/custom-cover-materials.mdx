    level: h2
    body: Cheap fabric fades and tears at caster contact points in one Southwest summer. Mesh used as a winter cover lets cold air straight through. Spec the fabric to your worst storage condition, not average weather.
  - heading: 'Solution: Pick by Condition'
    level: h2
    body: ''
    bullets:
      - Dry equipment yard, winter shutdown → 600D polyester + PU coating (default)
      - Rain/snow load on top, coastal or rooftop → vinyl-coated polyester
      - Idle in summer dust, unit still needs airflow → mesh (in-season only)
      - Freezing yard, months outdoors after full drain/dry → insulated liner option
