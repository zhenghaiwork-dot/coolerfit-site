sections:
  - heading: 'Problem: Wrong Closure for the Season'
    level: h2
    body: Mesh keeps dust off idle summer units while air flows. Used at winter shutdown, cold outdoor air passes straight through — drafts continue and rain soaks pads. Teams sometimes pick mesh because it is lighter and cheaper; the failure shows up as cold rooms and wet pads in spring.
  - heading: 'Solution: Closed for Shutdown, Vented for Idle'
    level: h2
    body: ''
    bullets:
      - Closed solid fabric — after full drain/dry shutdown; blocks drafts, rain, and pests
      - Mesh / vented — idle unit during cooling season in dusty yards; not winter
      - 'At shutdown: swap mesh for solid cover — do not leave mesh on through winter'
  - heading: Interior Duct Still Drafting?
    level: h2
    body: A solid cover seals outdoor intakes only. If rooms stay cold, check the interior duct damper or panel inside the house — cover fabric cannot fix an open duct path.
