sections:
  - heading: 'Problem: Dry Climate Fabric, Wet Climate Install'
    level: h2
    body: 600D PU polyester is the right default for dry Southwest winters — light rain beads off. On a rooftop where water pools after storms, or in coastal exposure, prolonged standing water wicks through stitched seams. Interior rust follows even when the cover "looks fine" from the ground.
  - heading: 'Solution: Match to Worst Case, Not Average'
    level: h2
    body: ''
    bullets:
      - Water-resistant (600D PU) — dry climates, wall mounts, light rain only
      - Waterproof (vinyl-coated) — rooftop ponding, heavy snow load, coastal rain
      - If you see puddles on top after storms → upgrade to vinyl regardless of climate zone
  - heading: Also Fix Drainage
    level: h2
    body: Wrong fabric is not the only cause of interior wet — a sagging top pools water even on vinyl. Crown the top with strap tension so runoff reaches edges.
