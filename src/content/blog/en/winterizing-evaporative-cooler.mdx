sections:
  - heading: 'Problem: Covering Too Early or While Wet'
    level: h2
    body: A cover on a wet unit traps moisture → rust, mold, and pad rot by spring. Shut down when nights stay below ~50°F and you will not need cooling again until spring. The cover is the last step, not a substitute for draining.
  - heading: 'Solution: Shutdown Checklist'
    level: h2
    body: Do these in order. Cleaning detail and storage tips are in the maintenance guide.
    bullets:
      - Power off at the disconnect — not just the wall switch
      - Close the water valve; disconnect and blow out the feed line
      - Drain the pan completely; leave the drain plug open
      - Scrub the pan; inspect pads — replace damaged ones or dry fully before covering
      - Seal the interior duct opening inside the house (damper or insulated panel)
      - Install cover — correct draft type, hem below lowest louver, tie-downs if exposed

  - heading: If You Already Have These Symptoms
    level: h2
    body: ''
    bullets:
      - Mold smell when you remove the cover → unit was wet; drain and dry fully next season
      - Cracked feed line in spring → line was still full of water when it froze
      - Still cold indoors → interior duct not sealed — cover alone does not fix in-house draft
      - Cover on the ground after a storm → add tie-downs; see installation guide
