sections:
  - heading: 'Problem: One-Season Fabric Failure'
    level: h2
    body: High-desert UV breaks down cheap polyester fast — especially at hem contact points and anywhere the fabric rubs on casters or roof curbs. Fading is the visible sign; brittleness and tearing at stress points follow in the same season.
  - heading: 'Solution: Spec for UV Exposure'
    level: h2
    body: ''
    bullets:
      - 600D or higher polyester with UV-stabilized PU or vinyl coating
      - Darker colors fade slower than light colors on exposed sites
      - Rooftop and open yard storage → shortest lifespan; inspect hems yearly
      - Store folded in shade between seasons when possible
  - heading: When to Replace vs Repair
    level: h2
    body: Small hem fraying on an otherwise sound cover → repair tape or patch. Widespread brittleness or coating breakdown → replace — UV damage is through the fabric, not just the surface.
