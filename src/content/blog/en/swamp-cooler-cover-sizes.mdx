    level: h2
    body: Stock charts list outside dimension ranges. Landing between two rows is common — especially when casters add height the chart does not show. Ordering down saves money once; reordering after a failed fit costs a season.

    level: h2
    body: ''

      - Between rows → pick the next size up; drawcord or strap takes up slack
      - Hem should sit below the lowest louver, not above casters
      - Still tight at hose ports → custom pattern, not the next stock size
