sections:
  - heading: 'Problem: The Cover Does Not Clear the Roof Curb'
    level: h2
    body: Down-draft describes conditioned air leaving through the cabinet base into a vertical duct. Intake louvers are commonly on the cabinet sides. A cover sized only to the visible box can bind at the curb, leave the lower cabinet exposed, or sag and hold water on an open roof.
  - heading: 'Solution: Measure the Cabinet, Base, and Exposure'
    level: h2
    body: ''
    bullets:
      - Measure maximum cabinet width, depth, and height above the roof curb
      - Record curb, flashing, service disconnect, water line, and any projecting motor housing
      - Use a reinforced top and a shape that encourages rain and snowmelt to run off
      - Anchor tie-downs to approved points without crushing panels or damaging the roof membrane
  - heading: Open Yard Storage
    level: h2
    body: Add tie-downs on exposed sites. Re-tension after the first storm — a sagging top is the main cause of pooled water and interior rust.
