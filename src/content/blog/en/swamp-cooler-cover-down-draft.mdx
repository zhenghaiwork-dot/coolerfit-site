  - heading: 'Problem: Water Pools or Top Intake Exposed'
    level: h2
    body: Down-draft units pull air through the top deck. Sides are mostly solid. A side-draft cover leaves the primary intake open and may sag in the middle — rainwater pools instead of running off.
  - heading: 'Solution: Down-Draft Profile + Drainage'
    level: h2
    body: ''
    bullets:
      - Wide, low cover shape — top panel is the critical seal
      - Reinforced top for rain and snow load in open yards
      - Tighten straps so the top crowns slightly — water runs to edges, not into louvers
      - Confirm outlet duct clearance before ordering — depth must clear roof connections
  - heading: Open Yard Storage
    level: h2
    body: Add tie-downs on exposed sites. Re-tension after the first storm — a sagging top is the main cause of pooled water and interior rust.
