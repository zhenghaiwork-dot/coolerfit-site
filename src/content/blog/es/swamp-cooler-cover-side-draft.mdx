sections:
  - heading: 'Problem: Side Intakes Still Open'
    level: h2
    body: Side-draft portables pull air through large louvers on the left and right faces. A cover that only seals the top leaves both intakes exposed — drafts and debris get in exactly where you thought you were protected.
  - heading: 'Solution: Identify and Wrap Side Draft'
    level: h2
    body: Large intake louvers on left/right; outlet on top or front.
    bullets:
      - Use a side-draft profile — tall enough to wrap both intake faces
      - Hem below the lowest louver edge, not mid-panel
      - Route drawcord or strap under the caster frame so wind cannot lift the hem
      - Do not substitute a down-draft cover — top seal alone fails on side intakes
  - heading: Still Gaps at Corners?
    level: h2
    body: Remeasure outside width and depth — pad dimensions are smaller than the cabinet. Wrong draft type with correct size still leaks.
