sections:
  - heading: Symptom → Cause → Fix
    level: h2
    body: Work top to bottom. If the fix does not match, remeasure before reordering.
    dataTable:
      headers:
        - Symptom
        - Likely cause
        - Fix
      rows:
        - - Cover blew off
          - Hem riding up over casters; no tie-downs
          - Route hem below casters; add ratchet straps to anchors
        - - Water pools on top
          - Sagging top; wrong draft profile
          - Re-tension straps to crown top; confirm down-draft cover on top-intake unit
        - - Mold smell in spring
          - Unit covered while wet
          - Drain/dry fully next season before cover; clean pan now
        - - Gaps at corners
          - Cover too small or wrong draft type
          - Remeasure outside W×D×H; match side vs down draft
        - - Fabric tearing at hem
          - Cover too small — stretched over hardware
          - Size up or go custom for port protrusions
  - heading: Still Stuck?
    level: h2
    body: Send photos of the installed cover — hem position, tie-downs, and any gaps — with outside dimensions. Most reorder issues are sizing or draft type, not fabric quality.
