sections:
  - heading: 'Problem: Open Intakes All Winter'
    level: h2
    body: Evaporative coolers need large intake panels to pull air through wet pads in summer. When the unit is off, those same openings become a straight path for cold outdoor air, rain, dust, and nesting pests. A tarp thrown over the top usually leaves side gaps — a fitted cover wraps the outside cabinet and closes every intake path.
  - heading: 'Solution: Match Cover Profile to Your Unit'
    level: h2
    body: Wrong type = gaps even when the size looks close. Identify your cooler first.
    bullets:
      - Side draft — tall cover; side louvers must be fully wrapped
      - Down draft — wide, low profile; top deck is the intake to seal
      - Rooftop — reinforced top + tie-downs; water must drain off, not into the curb
      - Window — frame-width fit; blocks the window opening from drafting
  - heading: Common Mistakes
    level: h2
    body: ''
    bullets:
      - Running the cooler with the cover on → overheating and motor damage
      - Covering a unit that still has water in the pan → rust and mold under the fabric
      - Using a down-draft cover on a side-draft unit → side intakes stay open
